# KaspaVerse branding policy

**The software is open source. The name and the mark are not part of that grant.**

KaspaVerse's source code is licensed under the ISC License (`LICENSE`). That licence is a grant of rights in copyright:
use, copy, modify, distribute. It says nothing about trademarks, and it is not a licence to use the project's name or
identity. This document is a project policy, not a legal instrument, and it creates no rights that do not otherwise
exist.

## Status, stated plainly

No trademark is registered. No legal entity holds these marks today. The project is maintained by its author; this
policy will be aligned with a company once one exists, rather than assert something that does not.

## What the project treats as its marks

The name **KaspaVerse** and confusingly similar variants; the KaspaVerse logo and the app icon built from it; product
and surface names used to identify the official project. The files that draw the mark are source code under the ISC
License like everything else. Using the resulting mark as a badge of origin is what this policy is about.

## What you may do

- Use, fork, modify and redistribute the code, commercially or not, under the ISC License.
- Say truthfully what your software is built from: "a fork of KaspaVerse", "based on KaspaVerse".
- Write about, teach, review or criticise the project by name, and show the logo when referring to the project itself.

## What the project asks you not to do

- Present a fork or derivative as an official KaspaVerse release.
- Use the name or mark as the name, icon or store listing of your own distribution in a way that suggests it is
  published, endorsed or reviewed by the project.
- Use the name or mark for a service, token, sale or fundraise implying official status. The software handles other
  people's money, and a user who trusts the wrong build loses funds.
- Modify the logo and still call it the KaspaVerse mark, or register the name or mark as your own.

## If you fork

Rename. Ship under your own name and icon, keep the ISC copyright notice and this project's attribution (`NOTICE.md`),
and state the relationship in your own words.

Questions about a specific use: open an issue at `https://github.com/Drcryptodee/kaspaverse-v3`.
